Act terrain (macro)

The act's starting and ending geometry: each mission interpolates between the two according to its rank. This is where the regular grid gives way to the organic. Weights are relative (zone patches are drawn from them); “mix” blends all three inside a single patch.

Distortion: each difficulty has its own range, and the mission's rank places it inside — min + (max − min) × progress.
Careful, the scale saturates. Measured mean neighbour count (a square has 4, a hexagon 6): 0% → 3.7 · 4% → 4.4 · 8% → 4.7 · 16% → 5.2 · 32% → 5.2 · 55% → 5.3 · 80% → 5.3. Everything happens between 0 and 16%; beyond that the eye sees no difference. At 0%, 72% of tiles have exactly 4 neighbours — a real grid.

Roster (campaign squad)

The characters deployed in this campaign's missions (persistent HP). A mission's player tiles serve as deployment slots.

Squad dialogues

Played at the start of a mission based on the deployed participants: if ALL of a dialogue's participants are present, it fires (the first one not yet seen in the list, one per mission). Otherwise it waits for a future mission where they are together.

Territory (geoscape)

Generates a coarse mesh (same squares/hexagons/pentagons, at a large scale). Click a cell to edit it, double-click to quickly rename it. ⚠ "Generate" re-meshes from scratch and resets the annotations.
